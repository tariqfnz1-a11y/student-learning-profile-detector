Welcome, Student!

This assessment helps you explore the ways you prefer to engage with information, understand concepts and practise new skills.

How to participate:
  • Answer all 16 questions honestly.
  • Select the option that best describes your preference.
  • There are no right or wrong answers.
  • Your results will suggest useful study strategies.
This is a self-reflection activity, not an intelligence test or a scientific diagnosis. Learning preferences are not fixed types. Students can benefit from combining different learning approaches.
